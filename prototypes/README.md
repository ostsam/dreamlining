# Dreamlining prototype takes

These HTML prototypes use realistic mock data and remain visibly badged because nothing is connected to Neon or real sessions.

## Binding direction

- `01-roomboard.html` is the binding participant experience. Builders reproduce its room-first hierarchy, phase rail, balanced next-contribution nudge, open browsing, private trail, contribution composer, contact gate, and monthly scheme.
- `04-admin-control-room.html` and `05-admin-login.html` are the binding facilitator companion surfaces. Builders reproduce the shared-password arrival boundary, session/QR creation, phase controls, aggregate coverage, and report-only private-content disclosure.

The client approved this direction after desktop and mobile in-app-browser review on 2026-08-30. `DESIGN.md` governs the visual and interaction system; `SPEC.md` and `EVALUATION.md` govern behavior and verification.

## Non-binding exploration references

- `02-dream-gallery.html` — gallery-first: the room is a browsable collection with adaptive ordering and an explicit seen-without-comment shelf.
- `03-guided-inbox.html` — focus-first: one dreamline at a time with a personal queue, while browse-all remains one tap away.

These two takes may inform individual patterns, but they are not alternate shells and must not override Roomboard’s hierarchy.

Open each file in a browser. The primary interactions are wired as small local state changes: phase tabs, public/private comment toggles, contact-request approval, and commitment selection.
